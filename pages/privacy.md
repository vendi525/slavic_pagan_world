## Cookies

The first time you visit, the site asks if you are okay with cookies. Your answer is saved in your own browser, so you only get asked once. You can change it anytime under "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

If you say no, the site sets no tracking cookies.

## Analytics

If you say yes to cookies, the site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. It shows things like your rough location, device and the pages you visited, but not your name or email. Google Analytics only loads after you say yes. If you change your answer to no later, it stops and its cookies are deleted.

Google processes this data for me. You can read more in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Newsletter

If you sign up, I save your email address, the date you signed up and the wording you agreed to. They are kept in a private Google Sheet, and the emails are sent from my own Google account. I use your email only to send the newsletter: the Slavic holidays once a month and a short story once a week.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Clicking it deletes your details from the list right away.

## Videos

Videos on the site load from YouTube only when you press play. Until then, nothing is loaded from YouTube. When you play one, YouTube's own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.

## Shopping

The shop buttons take you to Etsy. Orders, payments and shipping are handled there, under [Etsy's privacy policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/privacy). This site does not see your order or payment details.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log your IP address for security. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Your rights

You can ask me to show you, correct or delete the data I have about you, or to stop using it. Just email <a href="mailto:slavicfairyofficial@gmail.com">slavicfairyofficial@gmail.com</a> and I will reply within 30 days.

If you think I have handled your data wrongly, you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live. In Slovenia, that is the Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec).
