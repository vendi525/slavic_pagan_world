Hi lovely soul,

Here is an ancient anecdote for you to contemplate. This one is about escaping our fate. Below it is a list of Slavic pagan holidays for December.

Death in Tehran

A rich Persian was walking in his garden one morning when his servant came running to him, pale and shaking.

"Master, I have just seen Death standing among the trees, and he was looking at me. My time has come. Please, give me your fastest horse. If I ride all day, I can be in Tehran by nightfall, and he will never find me there."

The master gave him the horse, and the servant rode off as fast as it would carry him.

That afternoon the master walked into the garden and met Death himself. "Why did you frighten my servant like that?" he asked.

"I didn't come for him here," said Death. "I was only surprised to see him. You see, we are meant to meet tonight, in Tehran."

An old Persian tale, retold. Viktor Frankl tells it in Man's Search for Meaning.
